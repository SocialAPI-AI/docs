A platform app is the registration that a social platform requires before software can ask a person for access to their account. SocialAPI connects every platform except X, Telegram, and Bluesky through its own apps, so you register no app, apply for no app review, and keep no platform secret. X connects through an X developer app that you create. Telegram connects through your bot's token, and Bluesky through an app password.

Which app connects a platform changes only what you provide when you [connect an account](/guides/connect-accounts). It does not change the endpoints you call afterwards.

## Who connects each platform

| Platform | Slug | App that connects it | What you provide |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | `instagram` | SocialAPI's app | Nothing. The person signs in to Instagram and approves |
| Facebook | `facebook` | SocialAPI's app | Nothing. The person signs in to Facebook, approves, and you choose Pages |
| Threads | `threads` | SocialAPI's app | Nothing. The person signs in to Threads and approves |
| Google Business Profile | `google` | SocialAPI's app | Nothing. The person signs in to Google and approves |
| TikTok | `tiktok` | SocialAPI's app | Nothing. The person signs in to TikTok and approves |
| LinkedIn | `linkedin` | SocialAPI's app | Nothing. The person signs in to LinkedIn and approves |
| YouTube | `youtube` | SocialAPI's app | Nothing. The person signs in to Google and approves |
| Pinterest | `pinterest` | SocialAPI's app | Nothing. The person signs in to Pinterest and approves |
| Zalo | `zalo` | SocialAPI's app | Nothing. The person signs in to Zalo and approves for their Official Account |
| WhatsApp | `whatsapp` | SocialAPI's Meta app, through Meta's signup popup | Nothing to register. The person completes Meta's signup in a popup on your page |
| X / Twitter | `twitter` | Your own X developer app | The app's client ID and client secret, saved in the SocialAPI dashboard |
| Telegram | `telegram` | Your own Telegram bot, no app | The bot token from [@BotFather](https://t.me/BotFather) |
| Bluesky | `bluesky` | None | The account's handle and an app password |
| Trustpilot | `trustpilot` | Not connectable | Nothing. Trustpilot is not available yet |

Every platform in the table is open to every plan.

## Why X uses your own app

X bills API usage to the developer account that owns the app, in pay-per-use credits. X's free API tier is closed to new developer accounts. SocialAPI therefore connects X only through an app that you register, so every read and write on your X accounts is billed to your X developer account, not to SocialAPI.

You save one X app for your SocialAPI account and, optionally, a different X app for a single brand. Starting an X connection without saved credentials fails with `412 byok.credentials_missing`. [Connect X with your own app](/platforms/twitter/own-app) walks through the setup.

## Telegram and Bluesky have no consent screen

Telegram and Bluesky connect in one request. You send the credential in `metadata` on `POST /v1/accounts/connect`, and SocialAPI answers with the connected account:

- **Telegram:** a bot token. You create the bot with [@BotFather](https://t.me/BotFather). The token does not expire. It stays valid until you revoke it in @BotFather, including after you disconnect the account. See [Telegram](/platforms/telegram).
- **Bluesky:** the account's handle and an app password that you create in Bluesky's settings. Never send the account's real password. See [Bluesky](/platforms/bluesky).

## Permissions each platform asks for

Each platform's page lists the permissions that SocialAPI requests when the person connects, and what each one is for: [Instagram](/platforms/instagram), [Facebook](/platforms/facebook), [Threads](/platforms/threads), [Google Business Profile](/platforms/google), [TikTok](/platforms/tiktok), [LinkedIn](/platforms/linkedin), [YouTube](/platforms/youtube), [Pinterest](/platforms/pinterest), [Zalo](/platforms/zalo), and [X / Twitter](/platforms/twitter).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to register an app or apply for app review?">
    Not for Instagram, Facebook, Threads, Google Business Profile, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, Pinterest, Zalo, or WhatsApp. SocialAPI's apps already exist, and you connect through them. For X you create your own developer app, as described in [Connect X with your own app](/platforms/twitter/own-app).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Whose name appears on the platform's consent screen?">
    The name of the app that connects the platform: SocialAPI's app for every platform in the table except X, and your own X app for X.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use my own Meta, TikTok, or Google app?">
    No. Only X connects through your own app. Every other platform that has a consent screen connects through SocialAPI's app.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does my plan change which app connects a platform?">
    No. Plans change allowances such as brands and Facebook Pages, not apps. See [Plans](/guides/plans).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when a platform limits or revokes SocialAPI's app?">
    Every account connected through that app is affected at the same time. SocialAPI posts incidents on [status.social-api.ai](https://status.social-api.ai) and as `incident` notices in [Announcements](/guides/announcements). X accounts are not affected, because each one connects through your own app.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I handle the platform's access tokens?">
    No. SocialAPI keeps them and refreshes them. You send your SocialAPI API key. When a platform ends an authorization, the account's `status` changes and you reconnect it: see [Account status](/guides/connect-accounts#account-status).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
