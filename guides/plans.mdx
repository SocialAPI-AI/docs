Every SocialAPI plan includes every platform and every feature, with two exceptions listed below. Plans differ by allowances: how many brands you can create, how many posts and interactions you can send per billing period, how much media you can store, how long your history is kept, and how many analytics exports you can run.

A brand groups the connected accounts of one business, client, or project. Pricing pages call a brand a "profile": the two words mean the same thing.

Compare prices on the [pricing page](https://social-api.ai/pricing) and change your plan in the dashboard under **Billing**.

## Endpoints

| Method | Path | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `GET` | `/v1/billing/plans` | List every plan with its prices and allowances |
| `GET` | `/v1/usage` | Read your usage in the current billing period and your allowances |
| `GET` | `/v1/media/storage` | Read your media storage use and allowance |

## Plan allowances

| Plan (`tier`) | Name | Brands | Posts per billing period | Interactions per billing period | Media storage | Price per month | Price per year |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free (`free`) | Hobby | 2 | 10 | 50 | 100 MB | $0 / €0 | $0 / €0 |
| Starter (`starter`) | Side Hustle | 10 | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | $29 / €26 | $278 / €247 |
| Pro (`pro`) | Full Send | 50 | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | $109 / €97 | $1,044 / €929 |
| Business (`business`) | Empire | 200 | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | $349 / €311 | $3,348 / €2,980 |
| Enterprise (`enterprise`) | Enterprise | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Custom | Custom |

Every paid plan is sold in USD and in EUR; pick the currency at checkout. A subscription keeps the currency it started in, including when you change plan. 100 MB is 104,857,600 bytes.

What each limit returns when you reach it:

| Allowance | When you reach it |
|---|---|
| Brands | Creating another brand returns `403 billing.brands_limit` |
| Posts | The next publish returns `429 validation.post_limit_exhausted` |
| Interactions | The next reply or DM returns `429 validation.interaction_limit_exhausted` |
| Media storage | An upload that would exceed it returns `413 billing.storage_quota`. See [Media](/guides/media) |

Two features depend on the plan rather than on an allowance:

- **WhatsApp template messages** need a paid plan. On the Free plan, `POST /v1/platforms/whatsapp/accounts/{id}/send-template` returns `403 whatsapp.paid_plan_required`. Replies to people who messaged you first work on every plan. See [WhatsApp](/platforms/whatsapp).
- **Visual analysis in exports** (`include_vision`) needs a paid plan. On the Free plan, SocialAPI ignores `include_vision: true` and runs the export without it.

### Profile packs

On the Pro (Full Send) and Business (Empire) plans, your brand allowance grows in Profile packs of 50 brands. When your brand count reaches the scale threshold of your allowance (90% by default), SocialAPI adds a pack, within the pack budget you set. Packs you no longer need are released at the end of the billing period. Set the threshold and the budget in the dashboard under **Billing**. Each pack held raises `brands_limit` in `GET /v1/usage` by 50.

## Facebook Pages per brand

When `facebook_page_selection` is `true` on your SocialAPI account (`GET /v1/users/me`), each brand holds at most 2 active Facebook Pages on Free, 5 on Starter, 10 on Pro, 20 on Business, and any number on Enterprise. A Page is active when `is_active` is `true` in `GET /v1/accounts/{id}/pages`. Choosing or assigning more returns `403 platform.facebook.page_cap`, with the allowance in `error.meta.limit`. Pages cost no credits. When `facebook_page_selection` is `false`, no Page cap applies.

When `facebook_page_selection` is `true` and you change to a plan with a lower allowance, or a cancellation moves your SocialAPI account to the Free plan, SocialAPI sets `is_active` to `false` on the Pages of each brand above the new allowance, starting from the last Page added, and keeps the Pages added first. A Page with `is_active: false` cannot be published to.

## History and export allowances

| Allowance | Free | Starter | Pro | Business | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Event history in `GET /v1/events` | 1 day | 7 days | 30 days | 90 days | 90 days |
| Webhook delivery log | 3 days | 7 days | 30 days | 90 days | 90 days |
| Analytics exports per billing period | 2 | 10 | 20 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Videos per export | 30 | 200 | 500 | All | All |
| Wait between 2 exports of the same connected account | 7 days | 24 hours | 1 hour | 1 hour | None |
| Report link lifetime | 7 days | 30 days | 30 days | 90 days | 90 days |
| Transcripts (`include_transcript`) | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Visual analysis (`include_vision`) | No | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

Your SocialAPI account runs 1 export at a time on every plan. When an export cannot start:

- The billing period's export allowance is used up: `429 billing.export_limit`. It resets at the start of the next billing period.
- The same connected account was exported less than the wait above ago: `429 billing.export_cooldown`.
- Another export is still running: `429 billing.export_concurrent`.

Pinterest accounts cannot be exported on any plan: an export returns `400 account.exports_not_supported`.

## What counts as a post or interaction

Post and interaction credits are counted on plans with a post or interaction allowance (the Free plan). On plans where both are unlimited, nothing is counted and `posts_used` and `interactions_used` stay at `0`.

**1 post credit** for each of these requests:

- `POST /v1/posts` with `publish_now: true` or a `scheduled_at`. Saving a draft costs nothing.
- `POST /v1/posts/{pid}/publish`, which publishes a draft or a scheduled post. It costs 1 credit even when the post was scheduled with `POST /v1/posts`, which already cost 1 credit.
- `POST /v1/posts/{pid}/retry`.

A post costs 1 credit whatever the number of targets.

**1 interaction credit** for each of these requests:

- A public reply to a comment: `POST /v1/inbox/comments/{postId}`.
- A reply to a review: `POST /v1/inbox/reviews/{id}/reply`.
- A reply to a mention: `POST /v1/accounts/{id}/mentions/reply`.
- A DM: `POST /v1/inbox/conversations/{id}/messages`. One request costs 1 credit, even when it sends more than one message.
- A WhatsApp template: `POST /v1/platforms/whatsapp/accounts/{id}/send-template`.

**No credit** for everything else, including private replies, hiding, liking, or deleting a comment, reactions to messages, every read, and every account, brand, key, webhook, and Automation operation.

The same rules apply to [MCP tools](/integrations/tools) and to the actions an [Automation](/guides/automations) carries out: each costs what the equivalent API request costs.

### When a credit comes back

SocialAPI gives the credit back when:

- Any request that costs a credit fails with a `validation.*` error.
- `POST /v1/posts` fails with `publishing.validation_failed`.
- `POST /v1/posts`, `POST /v1/posts/{pid}/publish`, or `POST /v1/posts/{pid}/retry` fails with `429 publishing.velocity_limit`.
- Every target of `POST /v1/posts` with `publish_now: true`, or of `POST /v1/posts/{pid}/publish`, fails.
- SocialAPI refuses a reply or DM before sending it because the conversation's messaging window is closed (see [Direct messages](/guides/direct-messages)).

A comment reply, DM, review reply, or mention reply refused with `403 account.permission_missing` is never charged: see [Permissions](/guides/permissions). Any other failure keeps the credit spent. That includes a request the platform refuses (for example `429 platform.{platform}.rate_limit` on a reply or DM), a `409 resource.conflict` for duplicate content, a post that fails on only some of its targets, and a retry that fails again.

## Billing periods

Allowances reset at the start of each billing period.

- **Paid plans:** the billing period is your subscription's current period. It starts on the date you subscribed and renews with each payment.
- **Free plan:** the billing period starts at 00:00 UTC on the day of the month your SocialAPI account was created, and ends at the same time on that day of the next month.

`period_start` and `period_end` in `GET /v1/usage` give the exact bounds of the current period.

## Check your usage

`GET /v1/usage` returns what you used in the current billing period and your allowances. Any API key can call it, and it costs no credits.

```bash
curl https://api.social-api.ai/v1/usage \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $SOCAPI_KEY"
```

Response (HTTP 200) for a SocialAPI account on the Free plan:

```json
{
  "brands_used": 1,
  "brands_limit": 2,
  "profile_units_used": 1,
  "profile_units_limit": 2,
  "page_allowance": 2,
  "page_billing_exempt": false,
  "posts_used": 7,
  "posts_limit": 10,
  "interactions_used": 23,
  "interactions_limit": 50,
  "period_start": "2026-10-04T00:00:00Z",
  "period_end": "2026-11-04T00:00:00Z"
}
```

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `brands_used` | Brands in your SocialAPI account |
| `brands_limit` | Brands your plan allows, including Profile packs. `-1` means unlimited |
| `profile_units_used` | Always equal to `brands_used` |
| `profile_units_limit` | Always equal to `brands_limit` |
| `page_allowance` | Ignore this field |
| `page_billing_exempt` | Deprecated. Facebook Pages are not billed: ignore this field |
| `posts_used` | Post credits spent in the current billing period |
| `posts_limit` | Post credits per billing period. `-1` means unlimited |
| `interactions_used` | Interaction credits spent in the current billing period |
| `interactions_limit` | Interaction credits per billing period. `-1` means unlimited |
| `period_start` | Start of the current billing period (ISO 8601, UTC) |
| `period_end` | End of the current billing period (ISO 8601, UTC). Credits reset at this time |

`GET /v1/media/storage` returns your media storage: `used_bytes`, `limit_bytes` (`-1` means unlimited), and `count`, the number of stored files. Any API key can call it, whatever its scopes, and it costs no credits.

### Usage headers

Responses also report usage in headers, so you can track it without calling `GET /v1/usage`:

| Header | Sent on | Value |
|---|---|---|
| `X-Posts-Limit` | Requests that spend a post credit, on a plan with a post allowance | Post credits per billing period |
| `X-Posts-Remaining` | Same as `X-Posts-Limit` | Post credits left in the current billing period |
| `X-Interactions-Limit` | Requests that spend an interaction credit, on a plan with an interaction allowance | Interaction credits per billing period |
| `X-Interactions-Remaining` | Same as `X-Interactions-Limit` | Interaction credits left in the current billing period |
| `X-Storage-Used` | `GET /v1/media/storage`, `GET /v1/media/upload-url`, `POST /v1/media/upload`, and `POST /v1/media/{id}/verify`, on every plan | Bytes of media stored |
| `X-Storage-Limit` | Same as `X-Storage-Used` | Media storage allowance in bytes. `-1` means unlimited |

## When you reach an allowance

Over the post or interaction allowance, the request returns HTTP 429 and nothing is sent:

```json
{
  "error": {
    "code": "validation.post_limit_exhausted",
    "message": "Monthly post limit exhausted"
  },
  "request_id": "a1b2c3d4"
}
```

The interaction allowance returns the same shape with `validation.interaction_limit_exhausted`. Both reset at `period_end`. To continue before then, upgrade your plan.

`POST /v1/posts/import` checks the allowance before it creates anything. When the CSV holds more valid rows than you have post credits left, drafts included, it returns `429 billing.post_limit` and creates no post.

The posting velocity caps are separate from plan allowances: upgrading does not raise them. See [Rate limits](/guides/rate-limits).

## Change or cancel your plan

Change or cancel your plan in the dashboard under **Billing**. A cancelled subscription moves your SocialAPI account to the Free plan at the end of the billing period.

When a plan change lowers your allowances:

- Brands above the new allowance stay. Creating another brand returns `403 billing.brands_limit` until you are below the allowance.
- When `facebook_page_selection` is `true`, Facebook Pages above the new per-brand allowance stop being active, starting from the last Page added. See [Facebook Pages per brand](#facebook-pages-per-brand).

## Past-due accounts

Your SocialAPI account becomes past due when a subscription payment fails for the second time (the first charge and one automatic retry). While it is past due:

- **API:** every endpoint returns `402 billing.past_due`, except every method of `/v1/users/me` and every `/v1/billing/*` endpoint, which stay available so you can pay.
- **MCP server:** every request returns HTTP 402 with the code `billing.past_due`.
- **Webhooks:** SocialAPI delivers no events to your webhook endpoints. Events from this time are not queued, get no delivery record, and are not sent after you pay.
- **Automations:** Automations carry out no actions and record no Firings.
- **Platform events:** SocialAPI still records comments, messages, and other platform events. Read them with `GET /v1/events` after you pay, within your plan's event history.
- **Scheduled posts:** scheduled posts still publish at their scheduled time.

`GET /v1/users/me` shows the state: `billing_blocked` is `true`, `billing_blocked_at` is when it began, and `billing_block_reason` is `past_due`.

Pay the open invoice in the dashboard under **Billing** to restore access. Access returns when the payment succeeds, with no request to make. If every automatic retry fails and the subscription is cancelled, your SocialAPI account moves to the Free plan and access returns on the Free plan's allowances.

## Use from an AI agent

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `social_api_get_usage` | Returns the `brands_*`, `posts_*`, `interactions_*`, `period_start`, and `period_end` fields of `GET /v1/usage`. It omits `profile_units_*`, `page_allowance`, and `page_billing_exempt` |
| `social_api_get_storage_usage` | Returns bytes used, the storage allowance in bytes (`-1` means unlimited), and the number of stored files |
