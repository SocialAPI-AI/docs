SocialAPI does not support Trustpilot. You cannot connect a Trustpilot account, and no endpoint reads or answers Trustpilot reviews. The platform slug `trustpilot` is reserved.

## At a glance

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Platform slug | `trustpilot` |
| Auth | None. Trustpilot cannot be connected |
| Status | Not available |

## Capabilities

| Feature | Status | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Connect a Trustpilot account | No | `POST /v1/accounts/connect` with `platform: "trustpilot"` returns `403 billing.platform_not_available` |
| Read Trustpilot reviews | No | No account to read from |
| Reply to Trustpilot reviews | No | No account to reply from |
| Publishing, comments, DMs, mentions | No | Trustpilot has no such surface in SocialAPI |

## Reviews on other platforms

Google Business Profile reads reviews and sends replies through SocialAPI: see [Google Business Profile reviews](/platforms/google/reviews). [Supported platforms](/platforms/overview) shows which platforms support reviews.
