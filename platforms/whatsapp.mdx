SocialAPI connects a WhatsApp Business phone number through Meta's Embedded Signup and lets you receive and answer the messages that number gets through the [unified inbox](/guides/inbox). The number connects through SocialAPI's own Meta app, so you register nothing with Meta (see [Platform apps](/guides/platform-credentials)). WhatsApp has no posts, comments, reviews, or mentions.

## At a glance

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Platform slug | `whatsapp` |
| Auth | Embedded Signup (OAuth), on Meta's pages |
| Platform API | WhatsApp Cloud API |
| Status | Live. Available on every plan, except that sending template messages needs a paid plan (see [Plans](/guides/plans)) |

## Capabilities

| Feature | Supported | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Receive messages | Yes | Each message arrives in the inbox and as a `dm.received` event. See [WhatsApp messages](/platforms/whatsapp/messages#receive-messages) |
| Free-form reply | Conditional | Text, media, or an interactive message, only within 24 hours of the person's last message. Outside it, the send returns `400 whatsapp.window_closed`. See [The 24-hour service window](/platforms/whatsapp/messages#the-24-hour-service-window) |
| Interactive messages | Yes | Up to 3 reply buttons, a list of up to 10 rows, or 1 link button. See [Interactive messages](/platforms/whatsapp/messages#interactive-messages) |
| Template messages | Conditional | Create, list, edit, delete, and validate templates on every plan. Sending a template needs a paid plan: on the Free plan, `send-template` returns `403 whatsapp.paid_plan_required`. See [Template messages](/platforms/whatsapp/messages#template-messages) |
| Typing indicator and read receipts | Partial | `typing_on` and `mark_seen` work. `typing_off` returns `400 validation.action_unsupported` |
| Message reactions | No | Returns `501 resource.not_supported` |
| Message history | Partial | WhatsApp has no history API: conversations hold messages from the time the number was connected. A number that stays on the WhatsApp Business app also brings the chat history the business shares (see [Keep using the WhatsApp Business app](#keep-using-the-whatsapp-business-app)) |
| Phone number management | Yes | Request a code, verify, register, deregister, and read the status of the number. See [Phone numbers and business profile](#phone-numbers-and-business-profile) |
| Business profile | Yes | Read and update the profile people see in the chat |
| Publishing | No | WhatsApp has no posts. `POST /v1/posts` with a WhatsApp account returns `422 account.platform_unsupported` |
| Comments, reviews, mentions | No | WhatsApp has none of them |

## What you need

- A **WhatsApp Business phone number** that is not registered on the consumer WhatsApp app. A number the business uses in the WhatsApp Business app can keep running there: see [Keep using the WhatsApp Business app](#keep-using-the-whatsapp-business-app). During Embedded Signup you can select an existing number or create one.
- **Admin access to a Meta Business portfolio** (business.facebook.com).

You do not submit your own Meta App Review or complete business verification to connect, because the number connects through SocialAPI's app. The person connecting signs in with their Meta Business account, picks their WhatsApp Business Account and phone number, and the number is connected.

## Connect an account

WhatsApp connects like every other platform with a consent screen: your backend asks for an `auth_url`, you send your customer there, and they come back to your `redirect_uri` with the connected account. Meta's signup runs on Meta's own pages, so there is nothing to embed or host on your side and no domain to register: it works from any domain. Your API key stays on your backend the whole time. You can also connect from the SocialAPI dashboard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get the signup link (your backend)">
    ```bash
    curl -X POST https://api.social-api.ai/v1/accounts/connect \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer $SOCAPI_KEY" \
      -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
      -d '{
        "platform": "whatsapp",
        "redirect_uri": "https://app.example.com/oauth/callback",
        "state": "session_abc123"
      }'
    ```

    Response (HTTP 202):

    ```json
    {
      "auth_url": "https://www.facebook.com/v26.0/dialog/oauth?client_id=...",
      "state": "4a8f2c1e9b3d7f06a5c2e8b4d1f3a7e2",
      "message": "Open this link to connect your whatsapp account. Once you've authorized, the account will be connected automatically."
    }
    ```

    Always set `redirect_uri`: it is where your customer lands with the result, and the only way to receive the number choice described below. See [Redirect URI rules](/guides/connect-accounts#redirect-uri-rules). `state` is optional and comes back unchanged on the redirect.

    `onboarding` picks the signup Meta shows:

    | `onboarding` | Meta shows | Result |
    |---|---|---|
    | `cloud_api` (default) | The WhatsApp Business Account and phone number picker | The number is used through the API only. Use it for a new number or one already on the Cloud API. |
    | `business_app` | "Connect your existing WhatsApp Business app" | The number keeps working in the WhatsApp Business app too. See [Keep using the WhatsApp Business app](#keep-using-the-whatsapp-business-app). |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your customer to auth_url (browser)">
    Redirect the browser to `auth_url`, or open it in a new tab. Your customer signs in with Facebook, picks or creates their business portfolio, WhatsApp Business Account and phone number, and verifies the number if it is new.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the result on your redirect_uri">
    On success, your customer lands on your `redirect_uri` with the account appended:

    ```text
    https://app.example.com/oauth/callback?account_id=acc_01HZ9X3Q4R5M6N7P8V2K0W1J&platform=whatsapp&state=session_abc123&status=success
    ```

    `account_id` is the ID every WhatsApp call takes from here on. The number's messages now arrive in your inbox.

    On failure, the redirect carries `status=error` with `error` and `error_description`. The WhatsApp-specific values are:

    | `error` | Meaning |
    |---|---|
    | `whatsapp.no_waba` | No WhatsApp Business Account was shared during signup. Start a new connection. |
    | `whatsapp.no_phone_number` | The WhatsApp Business Account has no phone number yet. Add one in Meta Business Manager, then connect again. |
    | `whatsapp.number_banned` | Meta has banned this number. Connecting again does not help until Meta lifts the ban. |

    See [Redirect parameters](/guides/connect-accounts#redirect-parameters) for the full list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Choose a number

When the WhatsApp Business Account holds more than one phone number, the redirect carries `status=selection_required` and a `connection_id` instead of an `account_id`. Read the numbers with `GET /v1/accounts/pending/{connection_id}`:

```bash
curl https://api.social-api.ai/v1/accounts/pending/pc_4a8f2c1e9b3d7f06a5c2e8b4d1f3a7e2 \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $SOCAPI_KEY"
```

Response (HTTP 200):

```json
{
  "connection_id": "pc_4a8f2c1e9b3d7f06a5c2e8b4d1f3a7e2",
  "platform": "whatsapp",
  "brand_id": "1c9f6f0e-2b3a-4c5d-8e7f-0a1b2c3d4e5f",
  "expires_at": "2026-10-26T12:30:00Z",
  "profiles": [
    { "platform_account_id": "106540352242922", "display_name": "Acme Support", "username": "+1 555-010-0199", "profile_picture_url": "", "bio": "" },
    { "platform_account_id": "106540352242923", "display_name": "Acme Sales", "username": "+1 555-010-0200", "profile_picture_url": "", "bio": "" }
  ]
}
```

Each entry in `profiles` is one number: `platform_account_id` is its phone number ID, `display_name` the verified business name, and `username` the phone number. Connect one with `POST /v1/accounts/pending/{connection_id}/select`:

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.social-api.ai/v1/accounts/pending/pc_4a8f2c1e9b3d7f06a5c2e8b4d1f3a7e2/select \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $SOCAPI_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "platform_account_id": "106540352242922" }'
```

The response (HTTP 201) is the connected account. Choose within 30 minutes; after that the connection expires and returns 404, and your customer runs the signup again. Only the number you choose is connected.

### Keep using the WhatsApp Business app

To connect a number the business already uses in the WhatsApp Business app, and keep using it there, send `"onboarding": "business_app"` in step 1. Meta then walks your customer through linking the app, including scanning a QR code with their phone. The phone needs WhatsApp Business app 2.24.17 or later, and Meta decides during signup whether the number is eligible.

Once the number is connected:

- The chat history the business chooses to share in the app (up to 6 months) appears in its conversations as WhatsApp delivers it. Imported messages raise no webhook events, and their attachments carry `attachment_type` without an `attachment_url`.
- Messages the business sends from the phone app appear in the conversation and raise `dm.sent`, like messages sent through the API.
- Sending is capped at 20 messages per second, Meta's fixed limit for these numbers.
- The number is already verified and registered, so you never call `request-code`, `verify-code` or `register` for it: each refuses it with `409 whatsapp.business_app_number`. `status` returns `"business_app": true`, `"verified": true` and `"registered": true`. This also applies to a number connected from the dashboard, so read `business_app` from `status` to tell which kind of number an account holds.
- Meta refuses `deregister` for these numbers. When SocialAPI has no specific code for Meta's refusal, the call returns `503 platform.whatsapp.api_error`, and `error.meta.platform_code` holds Meta's numeric error code. The business disconnects the number from the WhatsApp Business app's settings.

## Messages

Receiving, replying, media, buttons and lists, the 24-hour service window, and template messages are on [WhatsApp messages](/platforms/whatsapp/messages). A free-form reply works within 24 hours of the person's last message, and an approved template reaches the person at any time.

## Phone numbers and business profile

These endpoints manage the connected number and its public business profile. On the phone number endpoints, `{account_id}` is the connected account ID. On the business profile endpoints, `{phone_number_id}` is the WhatsApp phone number ID.

| Method | Path | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `GET` | `/v1/platforms/whatsapp/phone-numbers/{account_id}/status` | Whether the number is verified, registered, and on the WhatsApp Business app (`business_app`) |
| `POST` | `/v1/platforms/whatsapp/phone-numbers/{account_id}/request-code` | Send a verification code to the number by SMS or voice call |
| `POST` | `/v1/platforms/whatsapp/phone-numbers/{account_id}/verify-code` | Verify the number with the 6-digit code |
| `POST` | `/v1/platforms/whatsapp/phone-numbers/{account_id}/register` | Register the verified number and set its 6-digit two-step verification PIN |
| `POST` | `/v1/platforms/whatsapp/phone-numbers/{account_id}/deregister` | Release the number from WhatsApp Business. The connected account stays until you disconnect it |
| `GET` | `/v1/platforms/whatsapp/phone-numbers/{phone_number_id}/business-profile` | Read the business profile |
| `PUT` | `/v1/platforms/whatsapp/phone-numbers/{phone_number_id}/business-profile` | Update the business profile fields you send |

A number with `registered: true` can send and receive messages. Keep the PIN you set with `register`: losing it means a 7-day wait before it can be reset. The request and response schemas are in the [API reference](/api-reference/introduction).

## Scopes

Embedded Signup asks the person connecting for these Meta permissions:

| Scope | Grants |
|---|---|
| `whatsapp_business_messaging` | Send and receive messages |
| `whatsapp_business_management` | Manage templates, phone numbers, and the WhatsApp Business Account |

The account permissions WhatsApp supports are `messages.read` and `messages.write`: see [Account permissions](/guides/permissions).

## Webhook events

| Event | When |
|---|---|
| `dm.received` | A person sent a message to the number. See [WhatsApp messages](/platforms/whatsapp/messages#receive-messages) |
| `dm.sent` | WhatsApp accepted a message sent through SocialAPI, or from the WhatsApp Business app |
| `dm.status.sent`, `dm.status.delivered`, `dm.status.read`, `dm.status.failed` | WhatsApp reported the delivery state of a message you sent. See [Delivery and read receipts](/platforms/whatsapp/messages#delivery-and-read-receipts) |
| `account.disconnected` | The number needs reconnecting. `reason` is `access_revoked` (the authorization was revoked, or Meta no longer serves the number) or `number_banned` (Meta banned the number) |

The payloads are in [Webhook events](/guides/webhook-events).
